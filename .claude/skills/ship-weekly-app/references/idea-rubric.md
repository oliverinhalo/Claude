# Idea scoring rubric

Score the top 8 candidates. Highest total wins. Write the table into the run report.

| # | Criterion | 0 | 3 | 5 | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ten-second value** — useful before any input | needs setup | needs 1 input | useful on load | ×3 |
| 2 | **Finishable in one run** — honestly, not optimistically | needs weeks | needs 2 runs | one run with room to polish | ×3 |
| 3 | **Repeat use** — they come back unprompted | one-and-done | occasional | weekly or daily habit | ×2 |
| 4 | **Zero running cost** — static or free-tier edge | needs a server | needs free DB | pure client-side | ×2 |
| 5 | **Findable** — a real search query leads here | nobody searches | vague interest | specific recurring query | ×2 |
| 6 | **Sellable later** — a plausible paid tier exists | no path | ads only | obvious pro feature or team seat | ×2 |
| 7 | **Not already free and good** — the incumbents are bad or bloated | solved well | solved okay | genuinely underserved | ×2 |
| 8 | **Shareable output** — produces a link, file, or image worth sending | nothing | screenshot | first-class shareable artefact | ×1 |
| 9 | **Interesting to build** — has one real problem worth solving | CRUD | mild | a genuine technical core | ×1 |

Maximum 90.

**Do not ship below 55.** If nothing clears 55, the ideation phase was lazy, not
the idea space. Go back to Phase 1 and generate 40 more — specifically in
categories the ledger has never touched.

## The question that kills most ideas

> If this existed and were perfect, would the person who needs it have already
> found something good enough?

If yes, the idea is a demo, not a product. Next.

## The question that saves good ones

> What is the one thing this does that the big incumbent *will not* do, because it
> conflicts with their business model?

No ads. No account. No upload of your data. Works offline. Exports in the format
they lock. This is where a one-week app beats a funded one, and it is the only
place it beats a funded one.
