# Idea bank

Append-only. Every candidate generated in any run lands here, shipped or not.
Rejected ideas are not deleted — they are the shortlist for a later week, and the
record of what was already considered.

Format: `- **Name** — who it is for and what it does. *(category)*`

Mark shipped ideas with `✅ <slug>` and rejected ones with `✗ <reason>` inline.
