# Design

Stage-lit instrument panel. Warm near-black in dark mode, warm paper in light.
Accent: brass/amber, used only for "what is sounding now". One typeface family (system sans)
plus monospace for pitch, cents and tab, where alignment matters.
Signature: the fretboard. A glowing note dot at the played fret with a ring that tightens as the
pitch gets in tune. Rosewood board, nickel fret wires, pearl inlays, in both themes.
Tokens live in `src/styles/tokens.css`; components use tokens only.
